# 🎬 CareerMentor AI — 3-Minute Live Hackathon Demo Script

This script provides step-by-step instructions for driving a flawless live demonstration of **CareerMentor AI** in front of hackathon judges.

---

## ⏱️ Demo Overview (3 Minutes Total)
- **0:00 - 0:30**: Introduction & Role Selection (`Home.jsx`)
- 0:30 - 1:15**: Skill Gap Analysis & Priority Matrix (`SkillGap.jsx`)
- **1:15 - 2:15**: Hero Feature — Interactive 5-Question AI Mock Interview (`Interview.jsx`)
- **2:15 - 2:45**: Customized 6-Week Action Roadmap (`Roadmap.jsx`)
- **2:45 - 3:00**: Executive Placement Dashboard Q&A (`Dashboard.jsx`)

---

## 📍 Step-by-Step Presentation Walkthrough

### Step 1: Introduction & Student Onboarding (0:00 - 0:30)
1. Open browser at `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Point out the **Spider-Verse cyber-neon design theme** and clean navigation header.
3. Show pre-filled profile:
   - **Student Name**: `Alex Vance`
   - **Target Role**: Select `Frontend Developer` from dropdown.
   - **Current Skills**: `HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React`
4. Say to judges: *"College students often struggle to know exactly what skills they're missing for campus placements. CareerMentor AI bridges that gap instantly."*
5. Click **"🚀 Analyze Skill Gap & Match Score"**.

---

### Step 2: Skill Gap Analysis & Priority Matrix (0:30 - 1:15)
1. Highlight the **Overall Placement Match Score (e.g. 65%)** and progress gauge.
2. Explain the Color-Coded Matrix:
   - **Green Checkmarks (✅)**: Acquired Skills (HTML5, CSS3, JS).
   - **Amber Warnings (⚠️)**: Partial Skills (React State & Hooks).
   - **Red Crosses (❌)**: High-priority Missing Skills (TypeScript, Testing, Web Optimization).
3. Filter by **"missing"** tab to highlight actionable gaps.
4. Click **"💬 Start AI Mock Interview Now →"**.

---

### Step 3: HERO FEATURE — AI Mock Interview Simulator (1:15 - 2:15)
1. Explain: *"Our hero feature simulates a live 5-question technical & behavioral interview."*
2. **Question 1**: Click the **"⚡ Auto-fill sample answer"** button (or type a quick 2-sentence response).
3. Click **"Submit Response for AI Scoring"**.
4. Show the live **AI Scorecard Card**:
   - **Score**: e.g., `9 / 10`.
   - **Constructive Feedback**: 2-3 sentence analysis.
   - **Strengths vs Areas to Polish** badges.
   - Point out the **Running Average Score Header** updates live.
5. Click **"Proceed to Question 2 →"** and repeat auto-fill for 1-2 more questions.
6. Click through to the **Final Interview Scorecard Summary** screen displaying overall average, key strengths, and growth recommendations.
7. Click **"🗺️ Generate My Custom Career Roadmap →"**.

---

### Step 4: 6-Week Career Roadmap (2:15 - 2:45)
1. Highlight the **3-Phase Chronological Milestone Checklist**:
   - **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2)**: Core Foundation & High-Priority Skill Gaps (TypeScript, React State).
   - **Portfolio Project Blueprint**: Concrete project idea to put on resume.
   - **Phase 2 & Phase 3**: Testing, Security & System Design whiteboarding.
2. Click **"⚡ Open Demo Executive Dashboard →"**.

---

### Step 5: Executive Dashboard & Q&A (2:45 - 3:00)
1. Show the **Executive Dashboard** unifying Profile, Match %, Interview Score, and Roadmap.
2. Demonstrate how judges can jump back to any step seamlessly via the **Navbar** during Q&A.
3. End with: *"CareerMentor AI gives every student a 24/7 personal placement coach in their pocket."*
